Outly Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Outly ("we", "our", or "us"). Outly is a plan-first, anti-flake social meetup mobile application designed to coordinate real-life small group outings with verified reliability and peace of mind.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in via Google or register an account, we collect your verified email address, full name, username, and profile picture (avatar).
  • Geographic Location: We collect your selected city/district (e.g. within Tamil Nadu or Indian states) solely to match you with nearby active outings. We do not track your live continuous background GPS location.
  • Dead Man's Switch & Safety Contacts: If you activate the safety features, the emergency contact name and phone number you provide are stored securely and used solely to compose emergency SMS/WhatsApp dispatch messages if you do not check in after an outing.
  • Device & Push Notification Data: We register your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token to send you time-sensitive outing notifications, host approvals, and show-up reminders.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To facilitate small group outing discovery and coordination.
  • To calculate your transparent Show-up / Flake Karma Score and reputation tier.
  • To send private ephemeral messages inside confirmed squad rooms.
  • To trigger automated Dead Man's Switch safety alerts to your designated emergency contact upon your explicit request.

3. Privacy & Ephemeral Squad Rooms

Squad rooms are private and restricted exclusively to confirmed members. Squad coordination messages automatically expire after the outing concludes.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We use standard, industry-leading infrastructure partners:

  • Google Identity Services: For secure Google authentication.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): For real-time push notification delivery.

5. Data Deletion & Account Removal

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated outing data at any time. To request complete removal of your data, please contact us at privacy@outlyapp.com.

Our Zero-Spam Guarantee

Outly is built for real human connections. We do not spam, display aggressive third-party ad networks, or sell data to advertisers.
